How we work.

One senior team from start to finish. Most engagements run three to four months, with brief to press-ready files in about 10–14 weeks.

  1. 01

    Brief & strategy

    We start with the product, the shopper and the shelf: who you're competing with, what has to read first, and where the brand needs to go.

  2. 02

    Concepts

    Distinct design directions, shown on real packaging formats — not just flat logos — so you can judge them the way a shopper will.

  3. 03

    Refine & visualize

    We develop one direction across every SKU and flavor, with 3D renders and mockups you can use for sell-in decks, ecommerce and launch.

  4. 04

    Production-ready files

    Print-ready artwork for your printer and co-packer, plus the assets you need online, from Amazon listings to social.

Questions, answered.

What does a CPG graphic design agency do?

A CPG graphic design agency creates the brand and packaging for consumer packaged goods: the identity, the packaging system across every SKU, product renders and the content that sells the product online. NutraMedia does all of this in-house for supplement, food and beverage brands.

How long does a CPG packaging project take?

Most projects take about 10–14 weeks from brief to press-ready files, within a three- to four-month engagement. Timing depends on the number of SKUs and whether you need a new identity or a packaging refresh.

How much does CPG packaging design cost?

It depends on scope: how many SKUs and flavors, whether the brand identity is new or existing, and whether you need renders, photography or Amazon content. Tell us what you're launching and we'll scope it.

Do you only work with supplement brands?

No. We design for supplements and nutraceuticals, beverages like energy and hydration drinks and coffee alternatives, and food and snack brands.

Do you design Amazon listing and A+ content?

Yes. We design listing images and A+ content, including for the entire Lemme line.

Do you work with brands outside the US?

Yes. We helped Applied Nutrition, one of the UK's biggest sports nutrition brands, launch in the US.
