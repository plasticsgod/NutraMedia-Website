Questions, answered.

What supplement packaging formats do you design?

Tubs, pouches, stick packs, gummy bottles and the boxes and displays around them — across every SKU and flavor in the range.

Can you redesign packaging for an existing supplement brand?

Yes. We've built new identities from scratch and reworked established brands, like Applied Nutrition's bold new approach for its US launch.

Do you design for co-branded or licensed flavors?

Yes. We've designed co-branded products with Tang and Chiquita for Applied Nutrition, making both brands read clearly on one pack.

Can you help us sell supplements on Amazon?

Yes. We design Amazon listing images and A+ content, built from the same system as your packaging.

How long does a supplement packaging project take?

It depends on how many SKUs and formats are in the range. We map out the timeline with you on our intro call.

Do you handle FDA label requirements?

We lay out every panel to fit the required elements — statement of identity, net quantity, Supplement Facts, ingredient list and manufacturer details — and flag anything that looks off. Final claims and compliance sign-off should come from your regulatory team or attorney.

Should we refresh our packaging or rebrand?

If people already recognize your brand, a refresh often does the job — like Redefine Foods, where we kept the logo and modernized everything around it. If the brand itself is holding you back, a full redesign like Fig Brew’s is the better move. We’ll tell you honestly which one you need.

Can you design for retail and Amazon at the same time?

Yes. We design the pack to read on shelf and as a thumbnail, then build listing and A+ content from the same system so both channels look like one brand.

What files do we get at the end?

Print-ready artwork on your printer’s dielines, 3D renders and the web and Amazon assets you need to launch.

Do you offer packaging consulting?

Yes — almost all of our clients also work with us as consultants. That can mean guiding them through the print packaging process, helping finalize designs they already have, or being a second set of eyes on big packaging decisions. For Alpha Lion, for example, we consulted through their print process and helped finalize existing designs.

Do you work with brands that are just starting out?

Yes — KRĒO came to us with an idea and no brand, and we built everything from zero.
