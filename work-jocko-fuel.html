Jocko Fuel Brand Work

Packaging for a brand built on discipline — a system that had to read as fast on a crowded energy shelf as it does in a gym bag.

Client
Jocko Fuel
Scope
Packaging
Category
Energy
Year
2024

The brief

Jocko Fuel came to us needing packaging that carried the brand's discipline-first ethos onto shelf without shouting. The category is loud: chrome, neon, aggressive type. The brief was to stand out by doing less.

What we did

We built a packaging system around a strict hierarchy — flavor, function, then everything else — so a single can reads in under a second at three feet. Type and color do the work; the layout stays disciplined across every SKU and scales cleanly as the line expands.
